## NCCU / CABank Taiwan Mandarin

The [official corpus description](https://talkbank.org/ca/access/TaiwanMandarin.html)
identifies naturalistic Taiwan conversations and 29 participants. Its citation
is Chui and Lai (2008), *The NCCU Corpus of Spoken Chinese: Mandarin, Hakka, and
Southern Min*, Taiwan Journal of Linguistics 6.2:119–144; DOI 10.21415/T5DT2T.

The linked transcript download returned an authentication page, not an archive.
No access barrier was bypassed and no account was created. TalkBank's current
[access description](https://talkbank.org/0share/access.html) requires registration
for transcript access. Its [ground rules](https://talkbank.org/0share/rules.html)
default to CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 and restrict redistribution of password-protected data.
Exact corpus permissions and independent reading annotations remain unverified.
Register as discovery/evaluation hold; do not bundle data in the app or repository.

## Sinica MCDC8

The [official source description](https://tmc.ling.sinica.edu.tw/corpus_list_en/)
distinguishes manually checked Pinyin/POS in MCDC8 from automatically processed
annotations in the other conversation collections. This makes MCDC8 particularly
relevant for evaluating complete and initial Pinyin without deriving references
from MinIME's own dictionary.

[Source access information](https://tmc.ling.sinica.edu.tw/corpus_resource_en/)
routes academic licensing through ACLCLP. No licensed transcript package is
present locally. Register as discovery/evaluation hold. Before use, establish
allowed evaluation/distribution scope, pin the original archive, split by whole
conversation, retain speaker/genre lineage, and audit transcript-reading alignment.

Neither a public metadata page nor Traditional Chinese spelling alone is evidence
of permission to redistribute transcripts or use them as production vocabulary.
The existing web n-gram pilot remains web-register evidence, not conversation data.

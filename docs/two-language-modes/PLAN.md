# Two-language modes: contract and evaluation

Behavior change based on 0.7.3 (3ecf81d). Chinese/English and the one-tap
English board remain. Taiwanese and Japanese each have exactly one secondary language. Benchmark Chinese
and English alternatives to inform the fixed product choice. Existing selections
resolve to English secondary after the completed balance comparison.
Core mode identity owns decoder scope, add-on scope, ranking and learning context.
The UI retains four mode tabs. Per the user clarification, no secondary-language
setting or extra pairing tabs. English is fixed for 台/日; alternatives remain in
the benchmark.

Focused-language full and incomplete candidates precede secondary-language choices,
including collisions with English words, contractions and Mandarin glyphs. Raw
recovery and explicit custom entries remain; password/literal fields, queued Space,
partial consumption, stale callbacks and held-candidate identity retain protections.
No per-word exceptions, new fitted scores or evaluation targets enter production.

Sequence: freeze 0.7.3 classes/assets and broad outputs; compare mode policy alone;
audit and expand source extraction separately; evaluate final dictionaries and
resource costs; shared-core/native gates before Android build and phone integration.
Existing source commonness labels and source frequencies do not establish complete
conversation coverage. Report missing readings, retrieval and independent genres
separately. Existing corpora are exposed regression/source-retrieval, not fresh gold.

Coverage inputs: docs/input-modes/coverage-inputs.tsv (3,922 conditions), Japanese
24,996 source-reading conditions and Taiwanese 15,489 source-reading conditions.
Report each genre/language/error condition and optional slots 1-3/1-8, availability,
and selected target. Ordinal slots are not physical row/page visibility. Measure
uncached lookup and callback latency separately from cached output auditing.
Risk: focus-first partial matches may displace the secondary language, as requested;
bigger indexes cost load memory/time, so evaluate before packaging. Pair metadata
must stay source-owned and no new Han spelling consensus is inferred.

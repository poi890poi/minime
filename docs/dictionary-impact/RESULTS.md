# Dictionary ordering, cost and apostrophes — 0.6.2

Baseline: 27f3f37 / 0.6.1. All production entries and frequencies remain
source-derived. No complaint words, phrase IDs or promotion weights were added.
The audit, source manifests, per-record desktop runs and phone component timings
are retained in this directory.

## Measured dictionary costs

Same phone RFCR91GWXLX, same component harness, four alternating-order trials,
512 synthetic saved entries, warm-up before timed lookups. Times are microseconds;
these measurements exclude Rime, touch dispatch and rendering.

| Component | Baseline p50 | Cache-only p50 | Final build p50 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Combine loaded pack indexes | 23,837 | 1 | <1 |
| Saved learned-phrase lookup | 9,177–11,860 | 46–342 | 47–345 |
| Saved manual-dictionary lookup | 777–937 | 1–2 | 1 |
| All-pack indexed lookup | 14 | 23 | 19 |

The old combine copied the entire index on the input thread. It now joins immutable
indexes by reference. Saved dictionaries were reparsed for each query; caches now
follow the actual saved string and invalidate on edits and deletion. Returned custom
lists remain defensive copies. The slightly slower composite lookup is a measured
negative result, small compared with the removed copying/parsing work.

Cold loading took 3.4–3.6 seconds on a worker, and approximate retained heap was
22–26 MB across runs (final: 3.63 seconds, 26.25 MB). No cold-load or memory
improvement is claimed. The final parser also classifies initial aliases. Loaded optional
packs remain cached until process exit, even after their switches are disabled.
These numbers demonstrate component improvements, not a measured frame-rate or
end-to-end typing improvement. See phone-baseline.json, phone-cached.json and
phone-final.json. A reported zero-microsecond median means below timer precision.

## Candidate ordering

Manual entries previously competed against unrelated decoder score scales, and
optional matches were always inserted after two guesses. Exact manual and learned
entries now lead. A full optional match can precede decoder guesses, while an
already leading, source-attested complete Chinese reading keeps priority.
One-letter-per-Han initial aliases retain the previous lower insertion boundary.
Raw input remains independently reachable; optional-pack ordering does not change
the Space default or train the base model when an extra candidate is selected.

The first blanket promotion attempt was rejected after broad testing: for example,
Chinese development full-input top-1 fell from 709/1000 to 657/1000, and initial-input
top-1 fell from 78/1000 to 36/1000 with every pack enabled. A small Wikipedia-only
improvement did not justify that regression. The corrected rule protects verified
base phrases and distinguishes abbreviated aliases. The earlier frozen/new
Wikipedia files and ordering-evaluation.json describe that intermediate experiment,
not the final release's results.

The evaluator also needed a repair: its simulated past choices followed the changed
candidate order and could select supplements, which intentionally do not train base
preferences. Priming now disables optional packs, keeping past choices identical
between experiments. Raw reports use this controlled setup.

## Apostrophe policy and data

In ordinary English text fields, source-backed missing apostrophes restore on Space
independently of the broader spelling-correction switch. Existing source-valid bare
spellings keep their spelling. Thus `cant` remains `cant`, and `can't` is the leading
alternative instead of being buried after unrelated completions. `Cant` preserves
its casing. Immediate Backspace recovers the raw spelling; explicitly choosing raw
teaches that preference. Private mode uses static source data, without personal
learning. Technical/literal fields retain their existing guards.

Validity uses all eligible AOSP source vocabulary, not just the suggestion list's
frequency-filtered subset. The 11,070-byte en_spelling.tsv records 727 collision
spellings and 52 grammatical contractions. It is reproducibly generated by
tools/compile_english_spelling.py from pinned AOSP and UD EWT **TRAIN** data. Entries
marked not_a_word in AOSP are excluded. Completion frequencies and model.bin remain
unchanged. Existing Apache-2.0 and CC BY-SA notices cover the derived metadata.

Pinyin restoration uses the source's AUX-expanded grammatical contractions. It does
not automatically promote possessives, letter plurals or apostrophized proper names
over Chinese initials. Broad validity metadata alone does not redefine Pinyin intent.
The rejected unrestricted attempt changed 50 Chinese Space outputs, including
initials becoming letter possessives. Those negative results are retained.

This is dictionary-backed restoration, not full grammatical disambiguation. A
spelling absent from the sources can still be misinterpreted, and English contractions
can collide with incomplete Chinese readings. Chinese alternatives and raw recovery
remain available. Optional vocabulary does not add a full Japanese/Taiwanese decoder,
and this release does not claim that all base Chinese ranking errors are solved.

## Verification

Core: 19,069 assertions, including 411 frozen continuity inputs, 96 source-derived
pack probes, 1,243 source apostrophe pairs, 48 sampled keys across casing/modes,
727 validity records and 52 annotated contractions. Counts are behavioral checks,
not language-model accuracy. Backspace in Pinyin originally failed the new undo
regression; the shared Space-acceptance path now records the reversible literal
commit in both modes. The failing log is retained.

The ordering-only native Rime run covers 13,014 frozen inputs / 21,044 records with
all four packs enabled. No English or Chinese Space output changed.

| Chinese dev full (1000) | 709 | 712 | 924 | 922 |

Top-1 improves modestly, while some later candidates move outside the first eight.
That reachability tradeoff is retained in the report, not presented as universal
ranking improvement. On the newly collected disjoint Wikipedia holdout, 141
eligible titles / 423 queries, full-input top-1 rises 23→24; initials remain 1 and
mixed input remains 6. All top-8 counts and Space outputs stay unchanged. The 59
unavailable titles remain in the coverage report; no production data was tuned to
misses. Full per-group results are in apostrophe-evaluation.json.

With ordering held fixed, restoration changes 127/130,233 English token observations
across fresh, primed and English modes. These are output changes, not independently
verified correction accuracy. Three Chinese mixed-reading observations change:
`hes` becomes `he's`, and `shes` becomes `she's` in both splits. The latter displaces
設施 as the automatic choice; it remains selectable. This is the explicit tradeoff
of restoring English contractions during mixed Pinyin input. No full-input,
initial-input or MOE-glyph Space output changes from apostrophe restoration.

On the separate GUM test corpus (1285 sentences, 15 genres), all 61,203 English token
observations across the three modes are byte-identical to baseline. Neither EWT
development/test nor GUM test text participates in spelling metadata generation.

apostrophe ordering/restoration/undo, dictionary cache invalidation, optional packs,

Samsung IME and MinIME preferences were restored; display state was verified as

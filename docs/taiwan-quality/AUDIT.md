# Taiwan coverage and first-choice glyph audit

Baseline: 3f25366 / MinIME 0.6.3. Two independent changes: source coverage for
non-geographic Taiwan sectors, and diagnosis/fix of rare first-choice glyphs.
The Rudy snapshot, geography asset, extractor and source attribution stay intact.

Source coverage must not be inferred from successful retrieval of existing entries.
Replace English-definition topic guesses with a reproducible encyclopedia category
graph import. Fully paginate each visited category; record roots, depth, unvisited
frontiers, redirects and failures. Do not restrict historical people by current
citizenship. Store dataset-level attribution and native source IDs, not handpicked
names or per-name article links. Readings must be source-attested or composed only
from unambiguous existing units; unresolved and non-Han names remain in the report.
Freeze coverage evidence before compiling. Distinguish inventory coverage from
pronunciation resolution and runtime top-eight retrieval. Existing Wikipedia
holdouts become overlapping coverage references once Wikipedia is imported; never
call them unseen language-accuracy tests. Keep optional switches and private fields.

Glyph audit extends beyond pair reversals: enumerate complete syllables, prefixes,
source-derived multi-unit initials/mixed inputs and partial native consumption.
Trace actual first visible result and Space acceptance through core, native, merge,
optional packs and composition. Use independent MOE/UD references only afterward.
Missing reference counts are unknown, not proof of rarity; distinguish alternate
pronunciations, rare-only syllables, incomplete-input prefix candidates and known
English overlap. No complaint-specific overrides or reference-trained weights.

Land only causal fixes supported by one-variable comparisons and broad frozen
conversation evidence. Core and desktop gates precede Android. Document negative
results, pronunciation gaps, cross-language effects, memory/load/lookup costs and
source bias. Keep independent commits. Phone RFCR91GWXLX only; restore IME and
preferences, sleep and verify display after every test session.

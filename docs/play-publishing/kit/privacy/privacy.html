MinIME Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

MinIME is an independently developed, offline Android keyboard. This policy applies to the MinIME app and its optional local language dictionaries.

Typing and local processing

MinIME processes the characters you type to compose text and suggest candidates. Text you choose to enter is sent to the app you are typing in. That app has its own privacy practices. MinIME does not send your typing to the MinIME developers or a prediction server. It has no Internet permission, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or keystroke log. To restore an interrupted composition, it may check its own composing text immediately before the cursor, limited to 96 characters; it does not collect the rest of the document.

Preferences and learned words

Settings and explicit candidate-choice preferences are stored in the app's private storage. Custom dictionary entries are saved when you choose Save. Optional Chinese phrase learning, English word-pair learning and recent emoji history are controlled in Settings. Taiwanese and Japanese choice learning can also be switched off. Disabling learning hides the applicable saved suggestions and stops new updates; it does not itself erase saved data. Static bundled dictionaries remain available independently of learning.

Sensitive fields

Password fields use direct input without composition, suggestions or learning. Fields marked by the receiving app as private or no-personalized-learning do not read or write personalized history. These protections depend on the field information supplied by that app. MinIME has no user account, server-side typing history or cross-device automatic synchronization.

Your controls and retention

Saved preferences and learned entries remain until you clear them, clear MinIME's app storage or uninstall it. Clear learned choices removes learned history and retains your manually saved custom entries. To remove custom entries, empty their list and save it. You can also clear repeated phrase history separately. Android automatic backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for MinIME's private data.

Optional manual backup and import

Export settings and learned words creates a readable, unencrypted JSON document only when you choose to export and select a location in Android's document picker. It includes your saved preferences, custom entries, candidate-choice counts, phrase history and recent emoji if present; it does not include a continuous typing log. The document may contain personal words. If you select a cloud document provider, that provider may upload/store the file under its own policy. MinIME does not upload it independently. Import reads only the document you select and replaces saved settings/history after showing a confirmation. Canceling the picker or confirmation leaves your saved preferences unchanged. Exported copies remain where you saved them even after MinIME is uninstalled; delete those copies through the document provider when no longer needed.

Other services

Opening the project's support or source pages uses your browser and those sites' own privacy policies. Distributing the app through Google Play also involves Google's store services; MinIME does not add an advertising identifier, account login or telemetry collection.

Policy questions

MinIME's public support and policy-question channel is https://github.com/poi890poi/minime/issues . Do not post passwords, private typing, or backup files in public issues. The Play listing will also provide the publisher's support contact when the app is published.

Changes

A future change involving network prediction, synchronization, advertising or different data use will require an updated policy and applicable user disclosures before it takes effect.
